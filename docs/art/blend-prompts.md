# Blend Am art: prompts for ChatGPT

Blend Am is played upright: an 8 by 8 board, with three pieces below it to drag on. In the new look the board is a tailor's wooden cutting table, the pieces are square patches of ankara and adire fabric, and a full line gets sewn up and lifts away. A tailor shows how you did on the finish screen.

The board is seen **from straight above**, like looking down at a table. The tools and the tailor are seen **from the front**.

Do every sheet in **one new chat**, so the style stays the same. Save each result into the `game images` folder under the name given. If ChatGPT gives a file its own name, just rename it.

## 1. Start the chat

Upload two Mama Put pictures from `game images` so ChatGPT copies the style: `Warm Cartoon Restaurant Counter Interior-1.png` and `Glossy Cartoon Trays of Fried Favorites.png`. Then paste this:

```
I'm making art for a mobile puzzle game set in a Lagos tailor's shop, Nigeria. The player fits square patches of ankara and adire fabric onto a tailor's cutting table. The two pictures I've uploaded show the approved style: glossy hand-painted cartoon, warm saturated colours, a clean dark-brown outline around every object, light from the top left.

Rules for every sheet I ask for:
1. Plain flat white background.
2. A wide empty gap between items. Nothing may touch or overlap.
3. A clear dark outline all round every item.
4. No text, letters, numbers, logos or brand names anywhere.
5. No shadows on the ground under items.
6. Items of the same kind are the same size and drawn from the same angle.

Reply "ready" and wait for my first sheet.
```

## 3. When you're done

Tell me when the four sheets are in `game images`. I'll cut them out, shrink them, and put them into the game:
- the fabrics replace the coloured squares, on the board and in the tray, one fabric per piece colour;
- the table becomes the board, with the chalk grid drawn on it in code so it always lines up;
- the sewing machine runs along a full line as it's sewn up, and the scissors snip when the table fills;
- the tape measure, pincushion and fabric stack sit around the board as small touches;
- the tailor goes on the finish screen: proud with the dress for a new best, measuring for a good game, scratching her head otherwise.

The stitching, the strip lifting away, the "+160" pop-ups and the sounds are done in code, on top of the art.
