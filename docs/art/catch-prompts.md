# Catch Am art: prompts for ChatGPT

Catch Am is played upright. Puff-puff, akara and peppers fall from the top of the screen, past a busy Lagos market, into a woven basket that you slide along the bottom. The food is seen **from the front**, as if you're standing in the market watching it fall.

Do every sheet in **one new chat**, so the style stays the same. Save each result into the `game images` folder under the name given. If ChatGPT gives a file its own name, just rename it.

## 1. Start the chat

Upload two Mama Put pictures from `game images` so ChatGPT copies the style: `Warm Cartoon Restaurant Counter Interior-1.png` and `Glossy Cartoon Trays of Fried Favorites.png`. Then paste this:

```
I'm making art for a mobile game set in a Lagos market, Nigeria. Puff-puff, akara and hot peppers fall from the sky and the player catches the food in a woven basket. The two pictures I've uploaded show the approved style: glossy hand-painted cartoon, warm saturated colours, a clean dark-brown outline around every object, light from the top left.

Everything is seen from the front, as if standing in the market.

Rules for every sheet I ask for:
1. Plain flat white background.
2. A wide empty gap between items. Nothing may touch or overlap.
3. A clear dark outline all round every item.
4. No text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere.
5. No shadows on the ground under items.
6. Items of the same kind are the same size and drawn from the same angle.

Reply "ready" and wait for my first sheet.
```

## 2. The sheets

**C1. The food** (save as `c1-food.png`)
```
One sheet, eight items in a 4-by-2 grid with wide gaps, all about the same size, seen from the front:
1. a round golden-brown puff-puff, slightly glossy, dusted with a little sugar;
2. a second puff-puff, a little darker and less round;
3. a third puff-puff, lighter and puffier;
4. a round akara (bean fritter), deep orange-brown with a craggy crispy surface;
5. a second akara, a little flatter and darker;
6. a magic golden puff-puff, shining gold with a soft glow and three small sparkles around it;
7. a bright red scotch bonnet pepper (ata rodo) with a green stalk, shiny and wrinkled;
8. the same red pepper tilted the other way, with tiny wavy heat lines rising from it.
```

**C2. The basket** (save as `c2-basket.png`)
```
One sheet, three items in a row with wide gaps, all the same size, seen from the front and slightly above so the opening at the top shows as a dark oval:
1. a wide round woven straw market basket, empty, natural tan straw with a darker rim;
2. the same basket, half full, puff-puff and akara peeking over the rim;
3. the same basket overflowing, piled high with puff-puff and akara, a few sugar crumbs around it.
```

**C3. The market** (save as `c3-market.png`)
```
A tall picture (portrait, 2:3) of an open-air Lagos market in bright morning sun, seen from the front. The top 15% is clear blue sky. Below it, a row of food stalls under big striped market umbrellas (red, blue, green, yellow) with trays of snacks, pepper and tomatoes on wooden tables, and a few blurry shoppers in ankara far in the background. The bottom 35% is a plain smooth sandy ground with a few pebbles, and nothing else on it. Keep the middle of the picture calm and simple, because food will be falling over it. No people close up.
```

**C4. The seller** (save as `c4-seller.png`)
```
One sheet with wide gaps:
1. Big: a cheerful Lagos puff-puff seller, a woman in a bright ankara blouse and head-tie, standing behind a big black pan of hot oil on a small stove, tossing puff-puff up into the air with a slotted spoon and laughing. Seen from the front. This is for the game's title and results screen.
2. The same woman, smaller, holding up a basket overflowing with puff-puff and grinning, for a win.
3. The same woman, smaller, fanning her mouth with one hand, eyes watering from a hot pepper, for a lost game.
```

## 3. When you're done

Tell me when the four sheets are in `game images`. I'll cut them out, shrink them, and put them into the game:
- the market becomes the background, with the sandy ground left for the basket;
- the food and peppers replace the drawn ones, with the three puff-puff and two akara mixed so it doesn't look repeated;
- the basket fills up as you catch (empty, half, overflowing);
- the seller goes on the results screen: tossing for a normal finish, grinning with the full basket for a new best, fanning her mouth when a pepper ended it.

The sparkles, crumbs, smoke, shake and the combo meter stay in code, on top of the art.
