# Reflex Training art: prompts for ChatGPT

Reflex Training is played upright, as a penalty shoot-out at night. You're the keeper: the striker runs up, and the moment he strikes the ball you tap to dive. The pitch is seen **from behind the penalty spot, a little above it, looking at the goal**. The keeper stands in the goal facing us; the striker is in front of us with **his back to us**.

Do every sheet in **one new chat**, so the style stays the same. Save each result into the `game images` folder under the name given. If ChatGPT gives a file its own name, just rename it.

## 1. Start the chat

Upload two Mama Put pictures from `game images` so ChatGPT copies the style: `Warm Cartoon Restaurant Counter Interior-1.png` and `Glossy Cartoon Trays of Fried Favorites.png`. Then paste this:

```
I'm making art for a mobile game: a penalty shoot-out at night in a Nigerian football stadium. The player is the goalkeeper. The two pictures I've uploaded show the approved style: glossy hand-painted cartoon, warm saturated colours, a clean dark-brown outline around every object, light from the top left.

Rules for every sheet I ask for:
1. Plain flat white background.
2. A wide empty gap between items. Nothing may touch or overlap.
3. A clear dark outline all round every item.
4. No text, letters, numbers, logos, badges or sponsor marks anywhere, including on shirts and the ball.
5. No shadows on the ground under items.
6. Items of the same kind are the same size and drawn from the same angle.

Reply "ready" and wait for my first sheet.
```

## 2. The sheets

**R1. The pitch** (save as `r1-pitch.png`)
```
A tall picture (portrait, 3:4) of a football penalty area at night under bright floodlights, seen from just behind the penalty spot and a little above it, looking straight at the goal. The goal is centred, its posts and crossbar white with a white net, and it spans the middle 70% of the width; the crossbar sits about a third of the way down the picture. Behind the goal: dark stands full of a blurry cheering crowd in green and white, and four tall floodlights glowing against a deep blue night sky. In front of the goal: bright striped green grass with the white lines of the six-yard box and the penalty box, and the white penalty spot near the bottom middle. No players, no ball, no people on the pitch, and no advertising boards with writing.
```

**R2. The keeper** (save as `r2-keeper.png`)
```
One sheet, five poses of the same goalkeeper with wide gaps, all drawn at the same scale, seen from the front as if we are the penalty taker: a young Nigerian goalkeeper with short hair, a bright yellow long-sleeved keeper's jersey and black shorts, big green goalkeeper gloves, and a determined face.
1. Ready in the middle: crouched a little, knees bent, arms out wide, bouncing on his toes.
2. Diving full stretch to his right (our left), body flat in the air, both gloves reaching out.
3. Diving full stretch to his left (our right), body flat in the air, both gloves reaching out.
4. Standing tall, holding a white football up high above his head with both hands, shouting with joy.
5. Sitting on the grass, legs out in front, hands behind him, looking up and laughing at himself.
```

**R3. The striker** (save as `r3-striker.png`)
```
One sheet, three poses of the same footballer with wide gaps, all drawn at the same scale, seen from behind (we see his back): a Nigerian striker in a red shirt and white shorts, red socks and black boots, short dark hair, no numbers or names on the shirt.
1. Standing still behind the ball, hands on his hips, looking at the goal.
2. Running in towards the goal, mid-stride, one arm forward.
3. Striking the ball: kicking leg swung through, arms out for balance, the ball leaving his foot.
```

**R4. The ball and bits** (save as `r4-bits.png`)
```
One sheet, six items in a 3-by-2 grid with wide gaps:
1. a classic white football with black panels, seen from the front;
2. the same football with short white speed streaks behind it, flying;
3. a silver referee's whistle on a black cord;
4. a pair of green goalkeeper gloves, palms facing us;
5. a green-and-white supporters' flag waving on a short pole, no writing on it;
6. a white football net bulging with a ball caught in it, seen from the front.
```

## 3. When you're done

Tell me when the four sheets are in `game images`. I'll cut them out, shrink them, and put them into the game:
- the pitch becomes the background of the tap pad;
- the striker stands, runs in and strikes, and the kick is the moment to tap;
- the keeper waits in the middle and dives to the side the ball goes, saving it or not by your time;
- the whistle shows for an early tap, the flag and the bulging net for the crowd's reaction;
- the keeper goes on the finish screen: ball held high for a new best, diving for a good average, sitting on the grass after a slow one.

The ball's flight, the dive, the crowd's roar and the timing are all done in code, on top of the art.
