# Ajo art: prompts for ChatGPT

Ajo is played upright: a 4 by 4 board where you slide naira notes, and two the same make the next one up. In the new look the board is a pink ajo contribution card, the notes are cartoon naira notes, and after ₦1,000 the tiles turn into piles of money. The ajo collector shows how you did at the end.

The notes, bundles and card are seen **from straight above**, lying flat. The collector is seen **from the front**.

**Important:** the notes must be clearly cartoon game notes, never copies of real money: no faces or portraits, no writing, no numbers, no coat of arms. I write the value on in code.

Do every sheet in **one new chat**, so the style stays the same. Save each result into the `game images` folder under the name given. If ChatGPT gives a file its own name, just rename it.

## 1. Start the chat

Upload two Mama Put pictures from `game images` so ChatGPT copies the style: `Warm Cartoon Restaurant Counter Interior-1.png` and `Glossy Cartoon Trays of Fried Favorites.png`. Then paste this:

```
I'm making art for a mobile puzzle game about ajo, the Nigerian savings club, set in a Lagos market. The player slides cartoon money notes on an ajo contribution card. The two pictures I've uploaded show the approved style: glossy hand-painted cartoon, warm saturated colours, a clean dark-brown outline around every object, light from the top left.

Rules for every sheet I ask for:
1. Plain flat white background.
2. A wide empty gap between items. Nothing may touch or overlap.
3. A clear dark outline all round every item.
4. No text, letters, numbers, faces on money, portraits, coats of arms or logos anywhere. The money must look like cartoon game money, not real banknotes.
5. No shadows on the ground under items.
6. Items of the same kind are the same size and drawn from the same angle.

Reply "ready" and wait for my first sheet.
```

## 3. When you're done

Tell me when the four sheets are in `game images`. I'll cut them out, shrink them, and put them into the game:
- the notes replace the coloured squares, ₦5 to ₦1,000, with the value written on each in code;
- the bundles take over from ₦2k (wad) to ₦1m (the safe);
- the card becomes the board, with its dashed day-boxes drawn in code so they always line up;
- the collector goes on the finish screen: handing over the bag for a new best, counting notes for a good game, shaking his head otherwise.

The slide, the merge snap, the PAID stamp, the floating values and the sounds are done in code, on top of the art.
